Anduril 2 Controls

An interactive map of the Anduril 2 flashlight UI. Tap a state to see every click it offers. Toggle between the Simple and Advanced interfaces.

1CC = click (press & release)
1HH = hold on the last press
3Cnumber = how many clicks
Tap a state to see its clicks. On the map, labels on the lines show the click that gets you there.

SwitchSimple ↔ Advanced

Turn the light off first, then:

  • 10HSimple → Advanced
  • 10CAdvanced → Simple

Which am I in? Battery check (3C) shows voltage once in Simple, repeatedly in Advanced.

ResetFactory reset

  1. Loosen the tailcap/head (cut power)
  2. Press and hold the button
  3. Tighten it back while still holding
  4. Keep holding until it bursts to full power, then release

Or, on some lights: from Off, 13H. Simple UI is enabled afterward.

MenusHow config menus work

Enter a menu, it blinks for each item. Hold on an item = skip it, release = enter it. Inside an item: click = +1, hold = +10. Stop pressing to exit.

Note: Some clicks ("some lights" / marked in the panel) depend on the model and compiled options — RGB aux LEDs, tint channels, the strobe set, factory-reset-by-button. The states and core clicks are the same across Anduril 2.
